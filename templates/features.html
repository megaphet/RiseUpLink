Platform Features

Core features designed to connect verified youth with opportunities.

Verified Profiles

Profiles include uploaded ID, transcripts, and recommendation letters that go through administrative review and optional field verification.

Donor Search & Filters

Donors and NGOs can search by category, country, camp/community and verification status to find suitable beneficiaries.

Opportunity Posting & Applications

Donors post scholarships, internships, mentorships and training opportunities — youth can apply directly through the platform.

Field Verification Workflow

When required, a field team performs on-site verification and records findings, which update the user's verification status.

Dashboards & Reports

Dashboards for youth, donors and admins track applications, verification status, and reporting metrics.

Secure Document Storage

Secure handling and storage of uploaded documents with access control and admin review processes.

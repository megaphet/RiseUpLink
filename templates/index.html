Why RiseUp Link

Many African graduates — especially those who are refugees, internally displaced, or living in underserved communities — are invisible to donors and opportunity providers. RiseUp Link provides a trusted way to verify beneficiaries and present them to donors and NGOs with transparency and dignity.

Problem Statement

There is no reliable, verified platform linking post-high-school youth to scholarships, internships, vocational training and mentorships. Verification is weak or absent, so donors cannot reliably identify genuine beneficiaries.

Platform Mission

Empower marginalized African youth by providing verified access to donors and opportunities, prioritizing equity, transparency and field-verified authenticity.
